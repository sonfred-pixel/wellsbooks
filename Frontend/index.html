About Wells Books ♥

📚 Your Literary Haven Since 2010
We're an independent bookstore passionate about connecting readers with great literature. Our carefully curated collection includes both new releases and rare finds.

Our Collection

  • Academic and professional books
  • Rare and collectible editions
  • Kenyan and African literature

Selling With Well's Books

Our Consignment Program

We offer a 60/40 split - you receive 60% of the sale price when your book sells. We handle all customer transactions, marketing, and shelf space.

What We Accept

  • Gently used books in excellent condition
  • First editions and collectible books
  • Local author works (special 70/30 split)
  • Complete sets and rare finds

Evaluation Process

Bring up to 20 books at a time to our shop during business hours. Our buyers will evaluate based on:

  • Condition (no markings, tight bindings)
  • Market demand
  • Current inventory needs

Pricing

We price competitively based on:

  • Online market comparisons
  • Book condition and rarity
  • Local market trends

Payment Schedule

Payments must be completed in full before the delivery of goods or services is processed. This ensures a smooth transaction and guarantees that all necessary arrangements for delivery can be made promptly. By finalizing payments in advance, customers can avoid delays and ensure that their orders are fulfilled efficiently and on schedule.
